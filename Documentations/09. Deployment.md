# 9 Deployment

## Deployment reference

The project records describe an outdoor prototype at the DTNTF UGM rooftop, third-floor roof level. This chapter describes operation with the 10 November 2026 firmware baseline. Confirm the actual unit, installation date, location, enclosure condition, and responsible operator at handover.

Existing image/reference blocks are retained for the owner's later revision. No additional deployment images are introduced here.

## Light sensor

Inspect BH1750 dome condition, shading, and contamination. Its output is lux; the baseline does not establish calibrated solar irradiance.

![Light Sensor Dome](../images/light_sensor_dome.jpg)

## Wind and rainfall installation

Align the vane with the recorded north reference and update/record the mechanical offset. The anemometer uses pulse input GPIO14; the UART header is reserved without a sensor. Confirm free mechanical motion, rain-collector level/cleanliness, and known tip response.

The ultrasonic zero reference in code is 88 cm and the water-level software cap is 85 cm. Verify actual geometry and sensor blind zone; these numbers do not certify the full physical range.

## Solar panel

Record panel rating, orientation, tilt, and shading for the actual site. The older record states a south-facing installation; do not treat this as proof of optimal orientation without site evaluation.

![Solar Panel Installation](../images/solar_panel_installation.jpg)

*Figure 8. Solar panel installation and orientation.*

## Power installation

The owner confirms SCC maximum 10 A, reported discharge setting 10.7 V, and MP1584 nominal 5 V / 3 A. Record the SCC model and verify that its disconnection protects the actual station branch. Follow that controller's specified connection sequence.

Measure supply at the source, converter outputs, controller rail, and USB modem during startup/transmission. A nominal rating is not a measured transient result. Check the switched USB return before attaching additional grounded connections.

![Power Installation](../images/power_installation.jpg)

*Figure 9. Installed solar-powered energy system.*

## Network configuration

Configure both WiFi profiles locally and ensure `MODEM_WIFI_SSID` exactly identifies the modem network intended for power recovery. Configure MQTT host, port, WSS path, base client ID, and topic. Keep passwords and sensitive service configuration out of the public repository.

The station firmware does not implement `SET_WIFI` from the separate OTA test. NVS-based provisioning remains future work.

## Startup procedure

1. Identify the board, harness, sensor models, and supply settings before applying power.
2. Verify polarities, level conversion, and converter outputs with a controlled hardware check.
3. Insert the prepared SD card and load the configured production sketch through USB.
4. Monitor Serial at 115200 when available; record sensor/SD initialization and reset reason.
5. Allow the configured 120-second modem boot wait before expecting the initial network scan. Measurements continue and early records may queue.
6. Confirm minute CSV output, network association, and a live PUBACK.
7. Confirm actual database storage and timestamp interpretation separately from LED/broker state.
8. Record measured voltages, failure events, firmware identity, and the test duration.

## Reading station status

| Indication | Meaning in the baseline |
|---|---|
| Blue blinking | Network connection or modem recovery phase |
| Green | Recent acknowledgement of the latest live record, healthy checked transport/state, no checked fault flags |
| Red | Unmet delivery/health conditions, a checked fault, or expired main-loop snapshot |

The LED worker expires a main-loop snapshot after 10 seconds, and live ACK freshness is limited to 90 seconds. A green station LED is not proof of a completed database write or full sensor health. Modem LED meanings depend on its exact model.

## Field logs and backlog

Use `data_minute.csv` for measurements, `delivery_events.csv` for delivery results, and `modem_events.csv` for recovery evidence. They are not a full capture of every Serial diagnostic.

The live outbox and backlog journal/checkpoints are operational state. Back them up together during controlled maintenance; do not delete one queue-state file independently while the station is active. The journal does not automatically shrink after draining.

After recovery, each successful live cycle permits one backlog attempt. Thirty records drain in about thirty successful pairs if no further faults intervene. Without working SD, the eight-record RAM backlog is limited and not power-loss persistent.

## Handover and maintenance

| Item | Evidence or maintenance record |
|---|---|
| Board and firmware | CAD/PDF/manufacturing identity, source version, configured build |
| Supply and modem | Loaded voltage, switching test, reset/recovery events, SIM/service status |
| Sensor assembly | Orientation, offsets, tip calibration, ultrasonic reference, cable labels |
| Enclosures and mounting | Inspection, sealing, corrosion, drainage, fastener condition |
| Storage | Capacity, backups, queue/checkpoint integrity, removal/maintenance history |
| Platform | Message ID/timestamp handling, database confirmation, duplicate handling |

Clean the panel, collector, moving wind parts, dome, and shield according to observed site conditions. Inspect after faults and severe exposure. Assign maintenance responsibility and record actual acceptance results; historical checkmarks are not new test evidence.
