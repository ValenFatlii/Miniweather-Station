# 1 Project Overview

## Purpose and baseline

The Miniweather Station is an ESP32 monitoring prototype that acquires environmental data, stores records on microSD, and transmits measurements to Hyperbase through MQTT over WSS. This documentation describes the 10 November 2026 production firmware baseline.

The objective is continuous field observation with local recovery of unsent records. Communication and storage failures have explicit limits; offline operation does not guarantee unlimited or lossless retention under every fault.

## Measurements

| Parameter | Device | Output meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Temperature, humidity, CO2 | SCD40 | Time-weighted mean over the reporting window |
| Pressure and altitude | BMP280 | Mean pressure and pressure-derived altitude |
| Illuminance | BH1750 | Mean lux, not solar irradiance in W/m2 |
| Wind direction | AS5600 | Circular mean angle |
| Wind speed | Pulse anemometer | Mean calibrated speed |
| Rainfall | Tipping bucket | Accumulation over the window |
| Local water level | JSN-SR04T | Mean level relative to the installation reference |
| Voltage and current | INA219 | Mean values at the installed measurement point |
| Electrical energy | INA219 | Integrated mWh over 60 seconds |

## Operating concept

Sensor sampling targets approximately one second. Measurement, JSON/CSV generation, and live delivery use 60-second windows. SCD40 values follow sensor data readiness rather than becoming new independent readings every second.

A live message is attempted first. A successful live PUBACK permits one backlog attempt; reconnecting does not drain the queue in a burst. Records use message IDs and measurement timestamps when the clock is synchronized.

The firmware can select from two configured WiFi profiles and can recover the USB modem through GPIO32 under a bounded recovery policy. A separate LED task expires a stale main-loop snapshot and checks recent live acknowledgements.

## Hardware and storage

The owner confirms a 12 V 8 Ah battery, SCC maximum rating of 10 A with a reported discharge setting of 10.7 V, and MP1584 converters rated nominally 5 V / 3 A. The SCC model/type and measured supply performance remain to be recorded. The anemometer uses pulses; the UART connector is reserved with no sensor installed.

MicroSD holds minute data, the persistent live outbox, the backlog journal/checkpoints, and delivery/modem event logs. Without functioning SD storage, the fallback backlog queue has eight RAM records and is lost on power removal. Capacity monitoring and controlled maintenance remain necessary.

## Project evaluation

The system supports educational and research monitoring. Calibration tables in this repository are historical results; deployment dates, firmware identity, test setup, and evidence must accompany new performance claims.
